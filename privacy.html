Privacy Policy — zvakendev publish

Effective: 24 November 2026

zvakendev publish is a personal tool of Yevhenii Zhvakin. It uploads and publishes the author's own videos to the author's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What data the tool uses

  • Access tokens issued by Meta, Google and TikTok when the author connects his own accounts.
  • The author's own video files, captions and the planned publishing times.
  • Basic information about the author's own accounts that the platforms return when publishing (account ID, published post ID and link).

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data of any other users.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the author's own computer and are used only to call the official APIs of Meta, Google (YouTube Data API) and TikTok. YouTube API use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of each platform (Google Account → Security → Third-party access; Facebook/Instagram → Apps and websites; TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps). After that, the tool can no longer publish.
